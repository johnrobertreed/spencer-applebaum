# Spencer Applebaum

**Spencer Applebaum** is an American investor. He is a general partner and co-head of venture at [Multicoin Capital](https://multicoin.capital/), based in New York. He joined the firm in 2018 as one of its first hires, became an investment partner in March 2023, and was named general partner and co-head of venture in March 2026. His work covers public-market thesis and coverage plus private-market underwriting for the firm’s funds.

## Career

In March 2026 Applebaum was named general partner and co-head of venture at Multicoin Capital, alongside Shayon Sengupta. Managing partner Tushar Jain wrote that the two would continue to help drive thesis formation and portfolio strategy across funds, and described them as among the primary authors, with him, of the firm’s updated 2026 investment thesis.

In March 2023 the firm named him an investment partner, again with Sengupta.

He joined Multicoin in 2018 as one of the firm’s first hires. His personal site says he helped grow the firm’s assets under management into the billions.

## Work

Applebaum works across public and private markets. In public markets he focuses on thesis formation, asset coverage, and on-chain diligence. In private markets he drives underwriting and negotiating for the firm’s funds, and leads deals across decentralized finance (DeFi), decentralized physical infrastructure networks (DePIN), and crypto-consumer applications.

His personal site describes him as one of the key architects of Multicoin’s updated 2026 investment thesis.
